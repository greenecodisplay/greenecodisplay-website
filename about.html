Company Profile

ZHANGJIAGANG GREEN ECO INDUSTRY CO., LTD is a professional commercial display equipment manufacturer dedicated to providing high-quality display racks, shelving, and complete store solutions to customers worldwide.

Our main products include supermarket shelves, clothing racks, cosmetic cabinets, display furniture, and store fit-out fixtures. We combine metal, wood, acrylic, and plastic manufacturing capabilities under one roof to serve as your true one-stop partner.

Located in Zhangjiagang, Jiangsu — near Shanghai port — we enjoy convenient sea export logistics. Our KD (knock-down) packaging design significantly reduces shipping costs for international buyers.
